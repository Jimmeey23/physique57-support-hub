## A1 · Priority + SLA model

| Priority | First response | Resolution | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | 30 min (15 min for Safety & Security) | 4 hr containment (2 hr) | Injury, harassment, fire/egress, data breach, staff theft, any `immediate_danger = Yes` |
| **High** | 2 hr | 12 hr | Booking/CRM outages, powerCycle/electric faults, member-facing billing errors, security devices, any field that says a class was blocked |
| **Medium** | 8 hr (same business day) | 48 hr | Routine member requests, maintenance, memberships, training feedback |
| **Low** | 24 hr | 5 working days | Backlog, ideas, requests, AMCs, SOP drafts, internal reports |

Two mechanical rules, not judgement calls:
1. **Volume-weighted front door** — any sub-category that drew ≥10 tickets in the historic log (Studio temperature/AC, class experience feedback, trainer punctuality, hosted-class swaps, class availability) gets a **2 hr first response** even at Medium/Low, because at that volume a slow first reply is what turns a query into a churn risk.
2. **Observed facts can only raise priority** — the same guard `inferPriority()` already implements: a reporter describing a dead AC as a "minor inconvenience" cannot defuse the ticket.
